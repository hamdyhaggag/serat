Justification for USE_EXACT_ALARM permission:

"Our application, Serat (صراط), is a Prayer Times and Adhan application. Its core functionality is to notify users exactly when the prayer time arrives. 
Religious prayer times are precise, and even a delay of a few minutes (caused by Android's Doze mode or battery optimizations) invalidates the primary purpose of the app.
Therefore, we require the USE_EXACT_ALARM permission to schedule exact notifications for Adhan at the specific prayer times calculated based on the user's location.
This aligns with the 'Calendar or Reminder' core functionality use case allowed by Google Play policies."
